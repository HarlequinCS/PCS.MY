## Where to Submit Your Sitemap

### 1. Google Search Console
**URL:** https://search.google.com/search-console

**Steps:**
1. Go to Google Search Console
2. Add your property: `https://projectcutisem.com`
3. Verify ownership (DNS, HTML file, or meta tag)
4. Navigate to **Sitemaps** in the left menu
5. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
6. Click **Submit**

**Direct Submission URL:**
```
https://search.google.com/search-console
```

### 2. Bing Webmaster Tools
**URL:** https://www.bing.com/webmasters

**Steps:**
1. Go to Bing Webmaster Tools
2. Add your site: `https://projectcutisem.com`
3. Verify ownership
4. Go to **Sitemaps** section
5. Enter: `https://projectcutisem.com/sitemap.xml`
6. Click **Submit**

**Direct Submission URL:**
```
https://www.bing.com/webmasters
```

### 3. Yandex Webmaster
**URL:** https://webmaster.yandex.com

**Steps:**
1. Go to Yandex Webmaster
2. Add your site
3. Verify ownership
4. Go to **Indexing** → **Sitemaps**
5. Enter: `https://projectcutisem.com/sitemap.xml`
6. Click **Add**

**Direct Submission URL:**
```
https://webmaster.yandex.com
```

### 4. Baidu Webmaster Tools (For China)
**URL:** https://ziyuan.baidu.com

**Steps:**
1. Register and verify your site
2. Go to **Data Introduction** → **Sitemap**
3. Submit: `https://projectcutisem.com/sitemap.xml`

## Important Notes

1. **Update Frequency:** Your sitemap is set to update weekly. Update the `<lastmod>` date in `sitemap.xml` whenever you make significant changes.

2. **Verification:** Make sure to verify ownership of your domain in each search engine before submitting.

3. **Indexing Time:** It may take a few days to a few weeks for search engines to crawl and index your site after submission.

4. **Monitor:** Check your search console regularly to see indexing status and any errors.
